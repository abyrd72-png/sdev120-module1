To complete this assignment section, identify and submit the five rules of pseudocode.

1. Write one statement per line. Each step of the process gets a separate line.

2. Capitalize keywords. Logic/Control terms get capitalized to stand out.

3. Indent. Indention shows the hierarchy of the structure.

4. End multi-line loops with a clear "END" to see where that section stops.

5. Write the pseudocode in plain English so anyone can understand, don't use actual code.
